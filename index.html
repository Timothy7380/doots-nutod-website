About DOOTS Nutod

Agro-Commodities Solutions That Deliver Real Value

DOOTS Nutod Nigeria Limited is an innovation-driven agricultural company headquartered in Ibadan. We combine traditional farming knowledge with modern agronomy (smart irrigation, drone mapping and soil analysis) to add value across the whole chain, from cultivation and processing to distribution and export.

01

Crop Production

Staple and cash crops grown with precision agriculture and sustainable techniques.

02

Cleaning, processing, packaging and branding of produce for local and export markets.

03

Premium raw and processed commodities supplied through an efficient supply chain.

04

Consultancy & Training

Advisory and capacity building for farmers, cooperatives and agribusinesses.
